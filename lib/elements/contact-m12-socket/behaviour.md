## Failure modes (condition → parameter it changes → symptoms)
| condition | parameter | visible (x-ray) | electrical | how found |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| spread | the springs no longer grip (bore Ø + 0.14): never better than marginal | an open socket mouth | that pole intermittent, worse with a cable that moves | wiggle test on that channel |
| corroded | contact resistance x 100 | a green socket | that pole high-r | volt drop across the joint |
